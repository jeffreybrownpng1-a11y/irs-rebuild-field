Privacy policy

IRS Rebuild Field, iPhone app. Last updated November 18, 2026.

IRS Rebuild Field collects jobsite information and files it in the Google Drive of the person using it. The app has no server of its own. Nothing the app collects is sent to the app's developer or to anyone else.

What the app collects

  • Room scans, made with the phone's camera and LiDAR sensor.
  • Photos, taken with the phone's camera inside the app. They are not added to the phone's Photos library.
  • Voice transcripts. When the user chooses to record a conversation, the audio is recorded on the phone and turned into text on the phone, using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio is not sent anywhere and is deleted once the text is saved.
  • The user's name, typed by the user. It is written at the top of the user's transcripts, and the user's initials go in photo file names.

Where it goes

Everything above is saved on the phone first and then uploaded to one place only: the Google Drive folder the user picked in the app. After Google Drive shows that a file arrived, the copy on the phone is deleted. The original of a room scan stays on the phone until the user deletes it. The app's files on the phone are left out of iCloud and computer backups.

Files in Google Drive are under the control of the owner of that Drive, and Google's own privacy policy applies to them there.

Google account and Google Drive

The user signs in with Google so the app can reach their Google Drive. The sign-in is kept in the iPhone's Keychain on that phone and is not shared with anyone.

The app asks for access to Google Drive and uses it only to:

  • list folders, so the user can pick their Customers folder and a customer;
  • add new files (scans, photos and transcripts) to the customer's folders;
  • add one new folder for each room scan;
  • read the names of photo files, and the room names in room-scan files that this app made, so the room list is the same on every phone.

The app does not edit, move, rename or delete files or folders that are already in Google Drive, and it does not read the contents of any other file.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not transferred to anyone.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No accounts or servers run by the developer.
  • No location collection.

Recording conversations

Laws on recording conversations differ from state to state. The person using the app is responsible for following the rules where they are.

Removing your data

Signing out in the app's Settings removes the sign-in and the saved customer list from the phone. Deleting the app removes everything it keeps on the phone. Files already in Google Drive can be removed there by the Drive's owner. Access can also be withdrawn at any time in the Google account's settings, under third-party apps with account access.

Questions

Use the support email address shown on the app's Google sign-in screen, or ask the person who invited you to the app.
