IRS Rebuild Field

An iPhone app for collecting jobsite information on rebuild jobs.

IRS Rebuild Field is used on jobsites by rebuild contractors and their crews. For each customer it collects three things and files them in that customer's folder in the user's own Google Drive:

  • Room scans. A LiDAR scan of the rooms, saved as a floor plan, a take-off spreadsheet and a 3D model.
  • Photos. Photos by room and by stage of the job: before, hidden damage, during and after.
  • Voice transcripts. A conversation is recorded, turned into text on the phone, and saved as a text file. The audio is then deleted.

The app works with no cell service. Files are saved on the phone first and upload to the user's Google Drive when service returns.

Who can use it

The app is not in the App Store. It is shared by invitation through Apple's TestFlight.

Google sign-in

The app asks the user to sign in with Google so it can file what it collects in the user's Google Drive. It lists the folders the user picks from, adds new files, and adds one new folder for each room scan. It does not edit, move or delete files that are already there.
